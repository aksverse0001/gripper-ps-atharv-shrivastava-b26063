# 2c - gripper

I'd use a **two finger linkage gripper** with slightly rubbery / textured contact pads. One servo moves both fingers inwards. It is a fairly simple setup and can hold cubes without using magnets, hooks, suction or glue.

| option | different shapes | grip | accuracy | simple build | total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| parallel jaw | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4.00 |
| two finger linkage | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4.00 |
| adaptive fingers | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3.70 |
| scoop / fork | 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 2.95 |

Weights: shape range 25%, grip 25%, accuracy 20%, ease 30%. Scores are rough and out of 5.

The parallel jaw is the runner-up. It is good for cubes, but the linkage fingers may be a bit more forgiving with the cylinder and cone.

The fingers should open wide enough for the 60 mm cube and the 60 mm cylinder. For the marker and loop, the finger shape needs to leave enough space to grab without dropping them. Textured pads increase friction, but the grip should not crush thermocol objects.
