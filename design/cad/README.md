# cad files

Open `robo_reach_chassis.scad` in OpenSCAD. Dimensions are in mm. It has the plate, four wheels, battery and controller blocks, front arm/gripper and the cube. Use F5 for preview and F6 to render. The animation variable can be moved from the Animate panel.

Main sizes: 240 x 180 mm plate, 170 mm wheelbase and 150 mm track. The extended and stowed STEP files show the two poses. STL is just a mesh of the model, not a complete printable assembly. For a real build, each part needs proper holes, bearings, tolerances and real motor/servo sizes.

`dimensions.svg` has the main dimensions and `exploded_view.svg` is a simple exploded view. The GIF is just a quick concept preview.
